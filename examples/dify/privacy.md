# Privacy

USDCtoFiat by Galleon Labs. Built on the public Peer/ZKP2P protocol.

This sideload plugin does not store wallet private keys and does not accept a
private-key credential. It talks to the public Peer/ZKP2P curator and indexer
to prepare unsigned cash-out transactions. Attribution is locked to
`peer-ref-TOFIAT` then `galleonlabs`.
